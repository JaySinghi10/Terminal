Help with Terminal

Email jay@terminalaero.com about anything, from a notification that didn't arrive to deleting your data. For a flight, include its number and date.

How often Terminal checks a flight

More often as departure gets closer. For a saved flight:

More than two days before it leavesevery 12 hours
From two days to six hours beforeevery 6 hours
In the last six hoursevery 10 minutes
In the last 90 minutesevery 2 minutes
In the airevery 5 minutes
From 30 minutes before landingevery 2 minutes

What Terminal sends notifications about

A saved flight gets a notification when:

  • its gate or terminal changes
  • it's delayed, or the delay changes
  • it takes off, and again when it lands
  • its baggage belt is announced
  • it's cancelled or diverted. A cancellation names the next flight on the route.
  • a delay leaves too little time for your next flight

If one doesn't arrive, check that notifications are allowed for Terminal in the iPhone's Settings. Profile in the app shows whether they are. If they are and it still didn't come, email with the flight number and date.

Adding flights from your email

In My Flights, tap the add button and choose Import from Gmail. The first time, it asks you to sign in with Google. Terminal reads flight confirmation emails from the last year and adds the flights it finds, with their confirmation codes. It has read-only access, so it can't change anything in your mail. The privacy policy says exactly what it reads.

Disconnecting your email

Open Profile and tap Log out. That deletes your sign-in record from Terminal's server and removes Terminal's access to your Google account. You can also remove it yourself at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Deleting your data

  • Everything on your phone: delete the app.
  • A flight on the server: remove it from your list. Its entry is deleted at once.
  • Your sign-in record: log out, as above.
  • Anything else: email jay@terminalaero.com. The server holds no name or email address, so include the flight numbers and dates on your list.

Is the flight information always right?

No. It comes from flight data providers and is shown as they publish it, so it can be late or wrong. The terms say this in full.

Does Terminal cost anything?

No. There are no ads, and no account to create.
